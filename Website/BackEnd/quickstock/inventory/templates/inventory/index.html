FAQ

Clear answers before you commit.

What is included in the 14-day trial?

The trial includes core Starter inventory tracking, basic sales logging, and a limited user seat to evaluate fit. Upgrade to Pro after 14 days to keep premium workflows such as exports, GCT reporting, roles, and multi-location tools.

What happens if the internet goes down?

QuickStock saves sales transactions locally during outages and syncs them to the cloud after the branch reconnects.

Does Pro include WiPay checkout?

Yes. Pro includes WiPay payment integration for secure online checkout flows.

How does GCT reporting work?

QuickStock JA generates GCT-ready reports with export options to help with monthly filings.

Can I cancel or downgrade?

You can downgrade at renewal. We keep your data accessible and exportable.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Pro includes CSV/Excel exports across inventory, sales, and audit logs.
